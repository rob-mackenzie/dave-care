---
title: Dave has lost his wallet
order: 1
---

Dave often puts his wallet somewhere safe and then forgets where. He may say someone has taken it. Stay calm, don't argue, and tell him you'll help him look.

## Where to look

1. His usual place: [where he normally keeps it]
2. Pockets of the coat, trousers or dressing gown he's wearing, and yesterday's clothes
3. Down the sides and back of his armchair
4. Bedside drawer, under the pillow and under the mattress
5. Kitchen drawers, cupboards, the fridge and the bread bin
6. The bin, before it's emptied

## If you can't find it

- There's a spare wallet in [where] with a little cash in it. Give him that so he feels settled.
- Write in the care log where you looked.
- Text [family contact name] so they know. **Don't cancel any cards.** The family will sort that out.
