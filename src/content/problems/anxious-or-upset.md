title: Dave is anxious, upset or confused
order: 3
---

Dementia can make Dave unsure where he is or what time it is, and that can be frightening for him.

## What helps

- Speak slowly and calmly, and sit at his level.
- Don't argue or correct him. Tell him you can see he's worried and that you're here to help.
- If he asks for [name], say [what the family would like you to say].
- Things that usually settle him: [his favourite music, a cup of tea, looking at the photo album, talking about ...].

## When to get help

If he's **much more confused than usual**, or it came on **suddenly**, he may be unwell. Sudden confusion can be caused by an infection. Call [NHS 111](tel:111) or his GP, and let the family know.

**If he's hurt or seriously unwell, call [999](tel:999).**
