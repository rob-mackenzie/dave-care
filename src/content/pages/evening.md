Tap each item as you go. The ticks are only on your phone and clear the next day.

## When you arrive

- [ ] Knock and call out before letting yourself in
- [ ] Say hello and tell Dave your name and that you're here to help
- [ ] Ask how his day has been and read today's notes in the care log

## Dinner

- [ ] Make dinner. Ready meals are in [the freezer / the fridge]
- [ ] Make a hot drink ([how he takes it])
- [ ] Check he's eaten what was left for lunch

## Medication

- [ ] Give his evening tablets from [the blister pack / the dosette box in the kitchen]
- [ ] Fill in the MAR chart

## Getting ready for the night

- [ ] Help Dave get changed for bed if he wants to. Pyjamas are in [where]
- [ ] Put his hearing aids on charge in [where]
- [ ] Put his phone on charge in [where]
- [ ] Leave a glass of water by his bed

## Before you leave

- [ ] Close the curtains
- [ ] Check the cooker, kettle and taps are off
- [ ] Leave the [hall / landing] light on
- [ ] Check the back door is locked
- [ ] Write in the care log
- [ ] Lock the front door and put the key back in the key safe
