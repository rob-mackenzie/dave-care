Tap each item as you go. The ticks are only on your phone and clear the next day.

## When you arrive

- [ ] Knock and call out before letting yourself in
- [ ] Say hello and tell Dave your name and that you're here to help
- [ ] Open the curtains and put the lights on if it's dark
- [ ] Check the house is warm. The heating should be at [temperature]

## Getting up

- [ ] Help Dave wash and get dressed. Clean clothes are in [where]
- [ ] Put yesterday's clothes in the laundry basket in [where]
- [ ] Make sure he has his glasses and hearing aids in

## Medication

- [ ] Give his morning tablets from [the blister pack / the dosette box in the kitchen]
- [ ] Fill in the MAR chart

## Breakfast

- [ ] Make breakfast. He usually likes [porridge / toast and marmalade]
- [ ] Make a cup of tea ([how he takes it])
- [ ] Leave a glass of water or squash next to his chair

## Before you leave

- [ ] Throw away any food in the fridge that's past its date
- [ ] Check the cooker, kettle and taps are off
- [ ] Put his wallet, keys and phone in [their usual place]
- [ ] Put the TV or radio on if he'd like it
- [ ] Write in the care log
- [ ] Lock the door and put the key back in the key safe
