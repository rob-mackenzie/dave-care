## Emergency

**[999](tel:999)** if Dave is hurt, seriously unwell or in danger. Then call the family.

## Worried about his health

**[NHS 111](tel:111)** when it's not an emergency, or call his GP during surgery hours.

- **GP:** [surgery name], [07700 900000](tel:07700900000)
